## Four report views

1. **Financial-loss overview:** report year and quarter filters; app category and state filters; headline KPIs; losses and cases by year/quarter; losses by scam type; application-level case counts, losses, and average loss per case.
2. **Demographic and platform analysis:** losses by age group; case distribution by acquisition channel; cases by platform type; filters for age group, gender, app category, and platform.
3. **State and ban analysis:** state map showing loss/case/recovery-related metrics; losses by state; cases by government ban reason; filters for ban status, state, and app.
4. **Complaints and recovery outcomes:** cases by complaint source and outcome status; year/quarter cases trend; case-level details including scam ID, app, state, amount lost, complaint source, and outcome status.
